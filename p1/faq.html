Frequently Asked Questions

  • Shipping
    What are the shipping times?

    Standard shipping: 5–7 business days. Expedited: 2–3 business days. Tracking is provided for all orders.

    How is order fulfillment handled?

    Orders are processed within 24–48 hours. You will receive an email when your order ships.

    Where can I track my package?

    Use the tracking number in your shipping confirmation or visit our Order Tracking page.

  • Returns & Warranty
    What is the return policy?

    Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery for most items. Items must be in original condition. See Return Policy for details.

    How do I start a return?

    Start a return from your account orders page or contact support via Contact.

    Do products have a warranty?

    Most products include a 1-year limited warranty. Warranty claims are handled through our support team.

  • Billing & Accounts
    What payment methods are accepted?

    We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and some regional payment options. See checkout for available methods.

    How do I cancel my subscription?

    Manage or cancel subscriptions from your account settings. Contact support for help: Support.

    What is the refund process?

    Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after we process the return.

  • Product & Troubleshooting
    How do plans differ?

    Compare plan features on the Pricing page. Each plan lists included features and limits.

    Sizing and fit information

    Check the product page size chart for measurements and fit recommendations.

    Basic troubleshooting

    Try restarting the product, updating firmware/software, and checking connections. For step-by-step guides see Help Center.
