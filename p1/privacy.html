Privacy & data protection

Privacy Policy

At StudentHub, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use StudentHub:

  • Name and email address.
  • Academic details provided by you.
  • Feedback and suggestions.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and time spent on the website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To improve our website and services.
  • To respond to your queries and feedback.
  • To provide educational resources and updates.
  • To maintain website security and performance.

Data Protection

We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Cookies

StudentHub may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. Some third-party services, such as analytics or hosting providers, may process limited data to help us operate the website.

Your Rights

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the StudentHub support team.
